# Greenex V2 — Bangladesh market reference notes

These notes explain how Bangladesh competitor research informed the V2 information architecture. The public Greenex demo **does not claim these companies' work as Greenex work**.

## Solar Electro Bangladesh Ltd. (SEBL)

Public source: https://www.solarelectrobd.com/

Useful portfolio patterns observed:
- Project/activity pages emphasize project location, capacity, installed equipment and client proof.
- SEBL publicly describes a 177 kWp solar mini-grid at Monpura, Bhola and 250 kWp mini-grid examples in Shariatpur.
- Their site also presents client names and project/gallery photography prominently.

V2 design lesson: Greenex Projects should show capacity/scope, client, location, services delivered, technical snapshot and gallery—not only a title and photo.

## Rahimafrooz Renewable Energy / Rahimafrooz Solar

Public sources:
- https://rahimafrooz-solar.com/
- https://rahimafrooz-solar.com/rooftop-and-ess/
- https://rahimafrooz-solar.com/dre-solutions/

Useful patterns observed:
- Solutions are grouped by application rather than only by product type.
- Rooftop solar messaging emphasizes design, engineering, construction and O&M as one chain.
- Distributed-energy pages show practical use cases such as solar irrigation, street lighting, mini-grids and productive-use systems.

V2 design lesson: Greenex should present "solution + execution + maintenance" together and keep product catalog separate from solution pages.

## Portable solar generator category reference

Public source: https://www.startech.com.bd/ecosonic

The Bangladesh market currently shows portable power station categories spanning roughly 300W to 3500W class devices, with LiFePO4 batteries and solar/AC charging common in the listed EcoSONIC products.

V2 design lesson: the Product dropdown includes Solar Generators, Panels, Inverters, Battery & Storage, Generators, Lift & Elevator, and UPS & Power Backup. The included Greenex demo products are generic sample entries, not EcoSONIC inventory claims.
